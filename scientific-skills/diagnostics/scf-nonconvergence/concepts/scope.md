# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** an electronic-structure output reports incomplete, oscillatory, divergent, or otherwise unaccepted SCF convergence.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** SCF convergence is not proof of the correct electronic state. Escalate state instability, severe spin contamination, suspected multireference character, or material model changes.
