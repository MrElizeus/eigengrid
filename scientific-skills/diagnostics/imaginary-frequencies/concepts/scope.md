# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** a frequency calculation reports one or more negative or imaginary modes for a molecular stationary point.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** A frequency magnitude alone cannot establish physical relevance. Escalate chemically meaningful distortions, multiple relevant modes, or transition-state claims.
