# Primary sources

## ORCA 6.1 manual, SCF stability analysis §2.16

A negative orbital Hessian eigenvalue signals instability within the probed rotation space. ORCA documents supported references, single-point context and restart behavior. [Source](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/essentialelements/stabilityanalysis.html)

## Radoń (2019), experimental benchmarks for iron complexes

Four iron complexes illustrate the functional and environment dependence of relative spin energetics. Transfer requires testing on the chemistry of interest. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1039/C9CP00105K)

## Wheeler and Houk (2010), integration-grid errors

Documents quadrature sensitivity in molecular reaction energies for several meta-GGAs and motivates refinement tests on the target energy difference. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1021/ct900639j)
