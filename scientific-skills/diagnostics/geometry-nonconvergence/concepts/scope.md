# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** an optimization reaches iteration limits, stalls, oscillates, fails coordinates, or ends without accepted convergence.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** More steps do not repair an inappropriate state or method. Escalate bond breaking, fragmentation, unintended isomerization, or changes to constraints and physical model.
