# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** a molecular electronic-structure objective can be addressed by a declared single-reference density-functional model.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** This unreviewed draft is not benchmark-backed and does not certify DFT accuracy for a chemical domain. Escalate suspected multireference character and material changes.
