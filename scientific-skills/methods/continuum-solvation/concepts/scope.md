# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** a molecular objective asks for solution-phase structure, energy, thermochemistry, or a solvent effect.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** Continuum solvation omits explicit microscopic solvent structure. Escalate strong specific interactions, mixed solvents, electrolytes, and accuracy claims beyond the declared applicability.
