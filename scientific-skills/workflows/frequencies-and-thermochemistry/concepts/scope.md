# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** a mission must validate a stationary point or derive harmonic zero-point and thermal corrections.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** The harmonic approximation can be poor for floppy modes and finite-temperature sampling. Escalate multiple relevant imaginary modes, low-frequency sensitivity, or requested high accuracy.
