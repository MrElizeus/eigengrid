# Primary sources

## Weigend and Ahlrichs (2005), balanced orbital families

Defines segmented basis sets at several valence qualities and evaluates their accuracy by element and property. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1039/B508541A)

## Rappoport and Furche (2010), diffuse augmentation

Tests property-optimized diffuse bases for response calculations. The choice of augmentation should follow the target observable. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20942521/)

## Boys and Bernardi (1970), counterpoise

Introduces evaluations in a common basis to reduce errors from basis borrowing in molecular interactions. [Source](https://doi.org/10.1080/00268977000101561)

## ORCA 6.1.1 manual, basis sets §§2.7.10–11

Explains overlap conditioning, removal of redundant vectors and fitting-basis roles. Inspect actual output for the installed version. [Source](https://orca-manual.mpi-muelheim.mpg.de/contents/essentialelements/basisset.html)
