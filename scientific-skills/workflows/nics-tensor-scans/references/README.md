# Primary sources and scope

- **schleyer-1996**. Schleyer et al. (1996) introduced the sign-reversed off-nucleus isotropic shielding as a magnetic aromaticity probe. [JACS](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja960582d)
- **stanger-2006**. Stanger (2006) studied how directional shielding varies with distance from the molecular plane and identified limits on assigning local aromaticities from NICS in polycycles. [JOC](https://doi.org/10.1021/jo051746o)
- **gershoni-poranne-2014**. Gershoni-Poranne and Stanger (2014) compared lateral profiles in multiring systems and investigated when total out-of-plane shielding approximated the qualitative pi-resolved result. The reported height is specific to those comparisons. [Chemistry – A European Journal](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.201304307)
- **stanger-2019**. Stanger (2019) examined height dependence and off-center effects on pi-resolved shielding in nonsymmetric systems. [JPCA](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.9b02083)
- **vist-2021**. Plasser and Glöcklhofer (2021) analyze shielding tensors and their orientations, including comparisons at ring centers and 1 Å above the plane. [European Journal of Organic Chemistry](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejoc.202100352)
- **orca-nics**. ORCA 6.1 manual §5.21.5 describes grid generation at probe centers, the very tight orbital function and auxiliary requirements. §5.21.6 covers orbital decomposition and its limitations. [Manual](https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.1/manual/contents/spectroscopyproperties/nmr.html)
