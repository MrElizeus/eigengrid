# Applicability and decision boundary

**Applies when:** a molecular wavefunction or density-functional calculation requires a basis, ECP, diffuse functions, or relativistic treatment.

**Does not establish:** benchmark accuracy, expert review, engine correctness, or authorization to execute.

**Material boundary:** Any change to charge, multiplicity, method, basis, physical model, or objective creates a new scientific decision and cannot be treated as an automatic retry.

**Escalate when:** This draft provides decision structure, not benchmark-backed basis recommendations. Escalate heavy elements, anions, weak binding, near-linear dependence, or precision claims.
